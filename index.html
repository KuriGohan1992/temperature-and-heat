Heat and Temperature

Heat is more than just something we feel, it’s energy in motion. Temperature tells us how fast the particles in a substance are moving, while heat explains how that energy flows from one object to another.

In this lesson, we explore how heat behaves, how we measure it, and why temperature isn’t the same thing as heat. This interactive page is paired with a short video lesson created by BSEMC (Batch 2027), covering topics such as temperature scales, unit conversion, heat transfer, and real-world applications. Whether you’re reviewing for class or learning it for the first time, you’re in the right place.
